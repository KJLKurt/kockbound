# Art direction

Established direction: polished colorful stylized 3D, expressive small humanoid creatures, elevated arena view, playful nonviolent chaos. The historical concepts are exploratory references, not locked character topology or exact UI. The current user explicitly requires phone play (D38); design and verify small-screen readability and thumb controls now. Historical mockups alone did not authorize that platform change.

Recovered references now include [all ten sheets](CONCEPT_ART_GALLERY.md): the original Sky Ring/Sprout concepts plus Lumi, expanded Sky Ring, Hill, Crown Chase, Cloud King, boss encounter, social lobby and mobile UI. Sprout's cream body/blue-pink fins and Lumi's blue long-eared silhouette/orange scarf are distinct explorations; compare both at gameplay scale before selecting the production base. Preserve their expressiveness, clothing-friendly anatomy and coherent blue/gold sky-world language. The sheets' proportions take visual precedence over an untested numerical proportion default. Historical logo text must be replaced with Knockbound in new production assets.

Use [the branch addendum](BRANCH_DESIGN_ADDENDUM.md) for attack/weak-point details, character variants and concept-to-rule reconciliation. The friendly Cloud King and imposing boss encounter treatment are alternatives to resolve, not a requirement to produce two bosses. Portrait phone concepts, team score meters and three-phase boss imagery must be assessed against the documented product scope.

## Character and shape language

Start with one distinctive creature whose clothing reads clearly. Large head, compact torso, oversized expressive hands/feet, readable face and silhouette. Avoid anonymous blob avatars, realistic anatomy and unrelated asset-pack characters.
The original 3.5-head numerical default is superseded for the current prototype by the recovered concept proportions: about 2.6 head heights excluding fins, two dominant masses, broad rounded forms and purposeful angular fin accents. See [the provisional selection and asset contract](CHARACTER_ASSET_CONTRACT.md). Final production identity, face and palette still require M3 review in the actual game.

Use one humanoid rig and standardized sockets. Skin hue and clothing are expression, not team recognition alone. Player number/name marker, ground ring and shape/icon reinforce identification. Rare effects cannot conceal attacks or increase apparent hit range. All outfits preserve collision fairness.

## World and camera

Sky Ring is an inviting floating arena: clear playable top surface, beveled perimeter, readable disappearing segments, restrained cloud/sky depth and a limited supporting architectural motif. Background decoration is subordinate to edges, opponents and danger. Hazard telegraphs precede activation and remain visible under cosmetics/VFX.

Start at 45 degrees downward, mild perspective, stable azimuth; tune within the recovered 35–55-degree range. Frame the whole actionable arena for local shared-screen play. Dynamic zoom must not lose players or obscure edge danger. Character showroom can use a closer camera, but gameplay asset review must use gameplay scale.

Map themes change strategy and art together. Magma Works uses warm industrial danger against safe readable surfaces; Frostfall separates traversable ice from lethal void. Do not rely on hue alone.

## Materials, light, color and UI

Use coherent stylized PBR-compatible materials, broad rough surfaces, controlled specular highlights and minimal tiny texture noise. Broad soft key light plus fill/rim where useful; contact grounding without muddy shadows. Avoid default flat lighting, excessive bloom and transparent fog over gameplay.

New palette roles: deep navy UI foundation, cream readable text, cyan primary action/player accent, warm coral danger and gold reward. Exact hex values remain exploration, not a brand lock. Theme variants preserve semantic danger/readability.

UI uses generous spacing, rounded deliberate shapes, one readable type family with clear weights, large primary Play/Play Again and concise cooldown cues. Match HUD: timer, remaining players/objective, dash and ability/item state. Economy/ads/XP live outside combat. Keyboard focus, scalable text, safe areas and touch targets must be considered. A busy neon dashboard is not the intended game UI.

## Animation and effects

Required base library: idle, walk/run, dash, hit, stunned, falling, eliminated, victory and two emotes. Clear anticipation and recovery, exaggerated weight transfer, squash/stretch in presentation and purposeful poses. Gameplay timing remains authoritative; root motion cannot change movement.

Dash has a directional trail; impact has brief local burst and reaction; dangerous knockback has stronger scale/sound, not longer screen obstruction. Telegraph, active and recovery states use distinguishable shapes and timing. Camera impulse and hit flash must be restrained, configurable and compatible with reduced motion. Local hit-stop affects presentation only.

## Production review

Before volume: front/side/back concept, expression and costume sheet, Sky Ring view, HUD/lobby/mobile layout exploration and boss scale sheet. Record selected reference IDs and what was retained or changed. No concept images were generated in this bootstrap.

Inspect turntable, gameplay view, four and twelve participants, bright/dark themes, smallest supported viewport, effect-heavy moments and cosmetic combinations. Reject clipping, unreadable silhouettes, accidental material variation, generic placeholder geometry at slice completion and inconsistent visual language. Temporary M1/M2 art is allowed and labeled.
