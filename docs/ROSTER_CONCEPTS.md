# Roster expansion concepts — 2026-09-08

Reversible art directions extending the recovered Sprout/Lumi family. Preserve compact proportions, large readable eyes, shared sneakers/gloves and the existing rest rig. Additional silhouettes must differ through authored geometry, not palette alone.

Pebble: woodland companion with two round ears, warm brown head/body, cream lower muzzle, dark small nose, short round tail and moss-green outfit. Rounded ear outline replaces Sprout's six-fan silhouette. Warm cream face must keep eyes readable at phone scale.

Wisp: lavender sky creature with two slender swept antennae and pale luminous tips, teal outfit and gold chest charm. Antennae replace the head fins; no new bones or gameplay reach. Face and hand/foot proportions stay compatible with the shared animation library.

Build both as separate Blender sources from the saved Sprout animated modules. Preserve the prior files, use inherited actions, export via existing validator and inspect actual renders before integrating. Color choices will be separate cosmetic-only selections for the expanded roster. No final visual or user approval is implied by this concept note.
