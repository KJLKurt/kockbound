# Optional hazard contract

Simulation/protocol/content 0.7.0 adds MatchConfig.hazards, selected from tiles/skyrock/gust, plus authoritative World.hazards schedule/rock markers/gust vector. Room create accepts validated hazards beside humanCount/items in both local and Worker adapters. Saved master/per-type Settings choose the next solo/host round; empty selection runs no hazard simulation. Client prediction does not invent hazards. Current preview defaults all three enabled, a reversible playtesting choice.

First hazard after ten active seconds, then every seven seconds; seeded selection among enabled types. Falling tiles choose an unwarned non-core tile using the support contract and warn one second. Falling rocks choose a live participant plus a small random offset, falling back to core if the target is unsupported. Red radius-1.6 m marker lasts 1.5 seconds; one impact pushes by 4 divided by mass and flattens/stuns for 0.8 seconds. Impact presentation remains half a second. Rock hits do not assign another player's credit. Wind chooses a seeded direction, warns one second, then adds acceleration 4 m/s² divided by mass for three seconds to all live players. Movement and impulse damping remain ordinary rules.

Hazard state uses bounded validated records; events hazardwarning/skyimpact drive cues and particles. Bots avoid upcoming rock markers and use existing tile avoidance. Red landing ring/fill, falling stone and dark teal directional arrows are original code geometry. Reduced motion preserves static arrows/markers and hides the pre-impact descending stone. These are initial tuning/presentation defaults, not phone/audio/human balance approval. Extra interactive danger and additional characters/colors remain required.
